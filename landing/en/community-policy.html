1.Manner Temperature System

To visualize users' trading reliability, the Company operates a Manner Temperature. Manner Temperature starts at 36.5°C and is calculated automatically by combining factors such as the user's transaction satisfaction, accumulated reports, response speed and keeping of appointments.

Temperature rangeMeaningVisibility policy
40.0°C or higherHighly trustworthy userTop-priority visibility
36.5–40.0°CRegular user (default)Normal visibility
30.0–36.5°CCaution neededReduced visibility weighting
Below 30.0°CSuspected abnormal tradingRestricted visibility + warning badge
Below 20.0°CSerious damage to trustworthinessTrading restricted + automatic review

Manner Temperature rises when transactions are completed and good reviews are received, and falls with accumulated reports, failure to keep appointments, no-shows and repeated cancellations.

2.Prohibited Items (No Trading)

Trading the following items is completely prohibited under applicable laws or Company policy.

⛔ Items prohibited from trading by law
  • Narcotics, psychotropic drugs, medical narcotics
  • Firearms, swords and knives, explosives, stun guns, crossbows
  • Organs, human tissue, blood, blood products
  • Obscene materials, materials violating child and youth sexual protection laws
  • Counterfeit banknotes, counterfeit gift certificates, forged ID cards, forged licences
  • Items suspected to be stolen or stolen property
  • Smuggled goods, goods exceeding duty-free allowances
  • Items violating the Wildlife Protection and Management Act (e.g. stuffed protected species)
⚠️ Restricted items (conditionally allowed / separate criteria)
  • Alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes — only between users verified as 19 or older (in-person handover recommended)
  • Prescription drugs, glasses/contact lenses — not allowed (violation of the Pharmaceutical Affairs Act)
  • Baby products (subject to recall) — may be traded only with the recall information clearly stated
  • Used cosmetics, perfume — unopened only; not recommended for hygiene reasons
  • Medical devices (e.g. blood pressure monitors) — only by businesses registered under the Medical Devices Act
  • Copyright-protected content (digital files without proof of authenticity) — prohibited
  • Imitations, knock-offs, luxury goods without a guarantee of authenticity — prohibited
  • Pets — rehoming/sale prohibited (restricted by law)

3.Prohibited Conduct (Use Restrictions)

Users must not engage in the following conduct.

3.1 Prohibited conduct related to transactions

  • Listing false items, listing items not in one's possession, or listing to manipulate market prices
  • Fraud, deception, taking payment or goods and disappearing (“hit-and-run”), unilaterally cancelling a transaction or going silent
  • Unilateral no-shows after making an appointment, or repeated requests to change the time
  • Trading gift certificates or gift vouchers to convert them into cash (simple transfer is allowed)
  • Luring users to external sites (attempts to obtain personal information, phishing links)

3.2 Prohibited conduct related to communication

  • Abusive language, threats, insults, hate speech
  • Sexual harassment, stalking, repeated unwanted contact
  • Disclosing other people's personal information (phone number, address, photos, etc.) without permission
  • Repeatedly sending promotional, advertising or spam messages
  • Inappropriate approaches to children and young people

3.3 Prohibited abuse of the system

  • Creating multiple accounts (to evade withdrawal or sanctions, or to receive rewards more than once)
  • Mass requests using automated bots, macros or crawlers
  • Hacking, abnormal access, exploiting vulnerabilities
  • Fraudulent accrual, duplicate receipt or laundering of rewards such as QTA
  • Bypassing area verification by manipulating location information (GPS spoofing)
  • Fraudulent manipulation of reviews, ratings or Manner Temperature

5.Sanction Levels (3 Levels)

LevelMeasureGrounds (examples)
⚠️ Level 1: Warning Warning notification + Manner Temperature −2°C + post deletion Minor advertising messages, inaccurate item description, failure to keep an appointment time once
🚫 Level 2: Temporary suspension Suspension of use for 3–30 days + Manner Temperature −5°C to −10°C + cancellation of transactions One instance of abusive language or insults, one suspected fraud case, one listing of a prohibited item, first detection of multiple accounts
⛔ Level 3: Permanent ban Permanent account suspension + blocking of device identifier + clawback of rewards (such as QTA) + blocking of re-registration Confirmed fraud, trading items prohibited by law such as drugs or firearms, inappropriate approaches to minors, repeated detection of multiple accounts, 3 or more accumulated Level 1/2 sanctions

Taking into account the seriousness, repetitiveness and scale of harm of a case, the Company may skip levels and apply a permanent ban immediately. Where a case is a criminal matter, the Company may be obliged to provide data to investigative agencies.

6.Appeals and Explanation Procedure

  1. A sanctioned user may file an appeal with the Company within 7 days of the date of notification.
  2. How to appeal: in the app via [Customer Center → Appeal a Sanction], or by email to quantarium1004@gmail.com.
  3. The Company will reply with the result of its re-examination within 5 business days of receiving the appeal.
  4. If the re-examination confirms that the Company's measure was unjustified, the Company will immediately lift the sanction and restore the Manner Temperature and any clawed-back QTA.
  5. If the user disagrees with the outcome of the appeal, the user may use the dispute mediation procedure in Section 8.

7.Transactions Restricted to Ages 19+ (Youth Protection)

  1. Trading items that fall under media/goods harmful to youth, such as alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes and alcohol-related products, is in principle limited to in-person handover between users verified as 19 or older.
  2. The Company operates keyword filtering, image screening and report-based monitoring to prevent young people from being exposed to items harmful to youth.
